SAVITRIBAI PHULE PUNE UNIVERSITY
T.Y. B.Sc. Statistics - Semester V
Paper ST-353: Biostatistics and Data Analysis
Credits: 4 (Theory 3 + Practical 1)

Course Outcomes: On completion the student will be able to summarise and
visualise real datasets, fit and validate predictive models, analyse time
series, and apply statistical methods to epidemiology and ecology data.

Unit I: Descriptive Statistics and Exploratory Data Analysis
Types of data and measurement scales. Measures of central tendency, dispersion,
skewness and kurtosis. Exploratory data analysis of a real dataset: data
cleaning, missing values and outliers. Data visualisation with histograms, box
plots and scatter plots using Python (pandas, matplotlib).

Unit II: Regression and Machine Learning
Simple and multiple linear regression, least squares estimation and residual
analysis. Logistic regression for binary outcomes. Introduction to machine
learning: training and test sets, cross-validation, overfitting. Classification
with decision trees and k-nearest neighbours. Model evaluation using accuracy,
precision, recall and ROC curves.

Unit III: Time Series and Forecasting
Components of a time series: trend, seasonality and noise. Moving averages and
exponential smoothing. Autocorrelation. Forecasting with ARIMA models and
evaluating forecast error on held-out data.

Unit IV: Epidemiology and Public Health Data
Measures of disease frequency: incidence and prevalence. Relative risk and odds
ratio. Cohort and case-control study designs. Analysing public health datasets
and disease surveillance data.

Unit V: Ecology and Biodiversity Data
Estimating population size: capture-recapture methods. Species abundance and
diversity indices. Field data from wildlife surveys and camera traps. Image
classification of species for biodiversity monitoring.

Practicals: Ten practicals in Python covering Units I to V using open datasets.

Suggested Reading:
1. Daniel, W. W. Biostatistics: A Foundation for Analysis in the Health Sciences.
2. James, Witten, Hastie and Tibshirani. An Introduction to Statistical Learning.
