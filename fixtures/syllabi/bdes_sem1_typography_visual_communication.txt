BACHELOR OF DESIGN (B.Des) - Semester I
Paper DES 104: Typography and Visual Communication
Credits: 4 (Studio 3, Theory 1)

Course Objectives: To build a working command of type, grid and hierarchy, and
to apply them to identity and layout problems for real audiences.

Unit 1: Fundamentals of Typography
Anatomy of type: baseline, x-height, cap height, ascender, descender, counter,
kerning, tracking and leading. Classification of typefaces: serif, sans-serif,
slab, script and display. Type measurement systems: points and picas. Legibility
and readability. Selecting and pairing typefaces for a brief.

Unit 2: Grid Systems and Visual Hierarchy
Modular grids, column grids and baseline grids. Margins, gutters and the type
area. Establishing visual hierarchy through scale, weight, colour and spacing.
Rule of thirds and the golden section. Designing a typographic scale. Responsive
layout principles for screen and print.

Unit 3: Colour Theory and Composition
Colour wheel, hue, saturation and value. RGB, CMYK and spot colour. Colour
harmony schemes: complementary, analogous and triadic. Accessibility and
contrast ratios. Gestalt principles of perception: proximity, similarity,
closure, continuity and figure-ground.

Unit 4: Brand Identity Systems
Logo design process: research, mood boards, thumbnails, refinement. Logotypes,
monograms and pictorial marks. Building a brand identity guideline document:
logo usage, clear space, colour palette, typography, imagery and tone of voice.
Designing stationery and social media template systems. Vector asset creation
and file preparation for print and digital delivery.

Unit 5: Editorial and Information Design
Magazine and report layout. Pull quotes, captions and running heads. Designing
an annual report spread. Infographic fundamentals: data-to-ink ratio, chart
selection and labelling. Preparing a portfolio case study.

Studio Work: Each student will produce a complete visual identity for a
not-for-profit organisation, including a logo, a one-page brand guideline and a
set of social media templates.

Suggested Readings:
1. Ellen Lupton, Thinking with Type.
2. Josef Muller-Brockmann, Grid Systems in Graphic Design.
3. Robin Williams, The Non-Designer's Design Book.
